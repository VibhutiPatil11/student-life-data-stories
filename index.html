About This Project

Hi, I'm Vibhuti Patil.

This project began with a simple question:

What are students actually experiencing behind grades, placements, deadlines, and achievements?

Using surveys, interviews, Power BI dashboards, and data storytelling, I explored five themes that repeatedly appeared in student conversations.

The goal was not simply to analyze data.

The goal was to understand people.
